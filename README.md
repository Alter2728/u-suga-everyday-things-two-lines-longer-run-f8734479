## The entry

> A shadow is your silent stunt double, copying every jump and wave on the pavement without ever touching anything.
> The double maps your shape and your motion, but it leaves out your colour, your weight, and the fact that it only exists where your body blocks the light.

Line 1 explains a shadow to a 10-year-old in one vivid sentence built on
a concrete, surprising analogy (a film stunt double). Line 2 says in one
sentence why the analogy works: it maps shape and motion, and leaves out
colour, weight, and the fact that a shadow only exists where a body blocks
the light.

## Gate

`/output/entry.txt` must be UTF-8 text of 1 to 400 characters, not only
white space. This entry is 2 lines, 1 sentence per line, well within the
limit (verified by the asserts in `main.py`).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and prints
the entry. Deterministic: byte-identical output on every run.
